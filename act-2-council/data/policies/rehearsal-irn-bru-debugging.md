# Proposal: adopt Irn-Bru as an official debugging tool

## Context

The team needs a harmless Proposal for rehearsing council timing, interruptions, and theatrical
verdicts before the live Glasgow Azure User Group presentation.

## Proposal

Place one can of Irn-Bru beside every developer during difficult debugging sessions. The can acts as
a visible signal that the developer is investigating a stubborn defect. Drinking it is optional.

## Claimed benefits

- The bright can makes blocked work visible.
- The sugar-free option supports teams with different preferences.
- A shared ritual might improve morale during long debugging sessions.

## Risks

- Correlation is not causation.
- Sticky keyboards remain outside the support agreement.
- No credible evidence shows that Irn-Bru fixes software defects.

## Decision requested

Approve, approve with conditions, or reject this rehearsal-only debugging standard.
