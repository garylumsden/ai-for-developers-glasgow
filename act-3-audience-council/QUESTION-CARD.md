# Audience Question Card

| Architect topic | Ask the room | Presenter decision |
|---|---|---|
| Premise | "What should this council decide, and for whom?" | Keep it light and legal |
| Branding | "Name it, and give me an emoji." | Take the first good shout |
| Document type | "What gets submitted to them?" | Choose one clear document type |
| Council | "Give me three or four characters." | Encourage conflict; allow 3–8 members |
| Grounding | Do not ask | Ungrounded |
| Tone | "How brutal, from 1 to 10?" | Keep it good-humoured |
| Sample dossier | Do not ask | The Architect writes one |

## If the room goes quiet

* Irn-Bru as a debugging tool
* Tabs versus spaces
* The Tunnock's teacake as a unit of story points

## Guardrails

* Avoid real people as personas.
* Avoid personal, medical, legal, political, or employment decisions.
* Keep the scenario suitable for a public community event.
* The presenter has the final decision on suggestions.
